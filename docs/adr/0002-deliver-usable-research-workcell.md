# ADR 0002 — Deliver the research workcell before formal cutting proof

Accepted2026-10-28 after the user clarified the priority is a built environment
that future research can continue and original strict stage thresholds may be revised.

Supersedes ADR0001's restriction on static workcell/API integration. Compose two
official Frankas and two native D455 hierarchies; provide live Newton/ideal-camera
environment and one-command recorded replay. Persistent cutting/RTX validation
does not block delivery of these independent tools.

Keep capability flags honest: a static arm is not articulated control, ideal RGB-D
is not RTX D455, deformation is not permanent separation. Force/oracle observations
remain excluded. No policy training or changes to other existing projects.
